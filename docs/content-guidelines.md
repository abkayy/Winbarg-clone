# Content & Writing Guidelines

This document provides rules for copywriting, content structure, and component text formatting to ensure a premium, professional, and consistent experience across the Winbarg Homes website.

## Tone & Voice
- **Professional & Premium:** The tone should inspire trust and convey high-end quality.
- **Clear & Concise:** Avoid jargon. Use active voice and straightforward language.
- **Authoritative:** Speak with the confidence of an established construction and real estate firm.

## Heading Hierarchy
- **H1:** Used only once per page (e.g., Hero section). Capitalized in Title Case.
- **H2:** Used for main sections (e.g., "Our Services", "Featured Projects"). Title Case.
- **H3/H4:** Used for sub-sections or card titles. Title Case.
- **Sub-headers / Eyebrows:** Small uppercase text with wide tracking (e.g., `uppercase tracking-widest text-brand-primary text-sm font-bold`) placed above main headings to provide context (e.g., "ABOUT US").

## CTAs (Call to Action)
- Buttons should use actionable verbs (e.g., "View Projects", "Contact Us", "Learn More").
- Maintain consistent button styles (Primary, Secondary, Outline).
- Button text should generally be Title Case or Sentence case.

## Section Structures
- **Hero:** Contains an H1, a brief descriptive paragraph, and 1-2 CTAs.
- **Content Blocks:** Keep paragraphs short (3-4 lines maximum) to improve readability.
- **Cards:** Ensure content within cards (e.g., Services, Projects) is uniformly sized to maintain grid alignment. Truncate text with ellipses if it exceeds the designated space.

## Capitalization Rules
- Always use Title Case for Navigation links and Footer links.
- Use Sentence case for body paragraphs and descriptions.

## Data & Stats
- Use large, bold numbers (`font-black`) to highlight key metrics (e.g., "15+ Years", "250+ Projects").
- Follow the stat with a clear, concise label in secondary text.
